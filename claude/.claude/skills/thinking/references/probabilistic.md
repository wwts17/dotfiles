# Probabilistic Decision

Decide under uncertainty using base rates, evidence updates, and expected value. Judge decisions by the quality of the process, not by a single outcome.

## Steps

1. Start from the base rate: how often does this kind of attempt succeed or fail historically?
2. Update on current evidence: what does this specific signal actually change, and in which direction?
3. Compare options by expected value, including the cost of the downside — not just the probability of success.
4. Set measurable triggers that would change the decision, and a stop-loss.

## Output

- Base rate: [historical frequency and its source]
- Update: [evidence, direction, and rough size of the shift]
- Options: [expected-value comparison, downside included]
- Decision and triggers: [the choice; revisit when X crosses Y; stop-loss condition]
